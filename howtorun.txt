Terminal menu options:
  6 - Queue a packet at a router. Repeat this to queue multiple packets.
      A router queue has capacity 10. The destination IP selects the
      destination router from routing_config.txt.
  7 - Execute one packet from a router. The packet with the highest
      priority is selected; ties are resolved by the lower packet ID.
      A forwarded packet waits in the next router's queue.
  8 - Break a direct link between two routers. The simulator rebuilds
      forwarding tables and connectivity, so queued packets use the
      updated routes when they are next executed.
  9 - Exit.

Example priority test:
  Queue packet 201 at R1 for 10.0.16.25 with priority 2.
  Queue packet 202 at R1 for 10.0.16.25 with priority 8.
  Execute R1; packet 202 is forwarded first.

To demonstrate a link failure, break R3-R4 and then execute packets at
the routers. Routes are recalculated around the failed link.

After running a packet simulation, open frontend/index.html and upload
simulation.json to replay the saved packet/stress-test animation.
